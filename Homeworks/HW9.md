$$(a^{\frac{m}{n}})^{\frac{1}{k}}=\sqrt[k]{a^{\frac{m}{n}}}=\sqrt[k]{(\sqrt[n]{a})^m}$$

And denote $\sqrt[n]{a}$ as $b$:

$$=\sqrt[k]{b^m}=(\sqrt[k]{b})^m=(\sqrt[k]{\sqrt[n]{a}})^m=(\sqrt[kn]{a})^m=a^{\frac{m}{kn}}=a^{pq}$$

Finally, $p=\frac{l}{k}$:

$$(a^p)^{\frac{l}{k}}=(\sqrt[k]{a^p})^l=((a^p)^{\frac{1}{k}})^l=(a^{\frac{p}{k}})^l=a^{\frac{pl}{k}}=a^{pq}$$
